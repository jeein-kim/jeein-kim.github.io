%% --- research interests ----------------------------------------------------
\cvsection{Research Interests}

Resolved neutral hydrogen (\textsc{Hi}) properties of local hard X-ray selected AGN host
galaxies; the effect of nuclear activity on the surrounding cool gas reservoir and
macro-scale (kpc--Mpc) gas feeding of Seyfert galaxies; \textsc{Hi}-bearing, low surface
brightness dwarf galaxies. Primary tools are radio interferometric \textsc{Hi} line and
continuum observations (VLA, ATCA, WSRT), complemented by optical spectroscopy
(Gemini/GMOS).
