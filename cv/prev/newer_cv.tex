\cvsection{Research Interests}
I study galaxy evolution through neutral atomic hydrogen (\HI{}), asking how galaxies gather, keep and lose the gas that fuels both the galaxies themselves and their active galactic nuclei (AGN) on kpc-to-Mpc scales. Throughout my PhD I have worked directly from visibilities with three connected arrays (VLA, ATCA and GMRT), from calibration and continuum subtraction to imaging, source finding, verification and kinematic modeling. I have also worked with \HI\ data from almost all connected arrays (e.g., WSRT, MeerKAT and ASKAP).

\vspace{3mm}

\noindent\textbf{Science.} Galaxy evolution, cold atomic gas (\HI{}), gas accretion and AGN fueling (kpc--Mpc scales), \HI{} absorption toward AGN, low surface brightness and optically dark galaxies, resolved \HI{} kinematics, SKA-era \HI{} surveys.\\[0.5ex]
\noindent\textbf{Methods.} Interferometric \HI{} line and continuum imaging, 3D kinematic modeling, \HI{} source finding, optical long-slit spectroscopy.
